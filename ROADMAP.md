# Roadmap

- **V1.1 Advanced Number Theory:** Möbius, sieve extensions, Lucas theorem,
  modular combinatorics.
- **V1.2 Graph Laboratory:** Bellman–Ford, Floyd–Warshall, MST, SCC, LCA.
- **V1.3 Data Structures:** sparse table, lazy segment tree, trie, monotonic tools.
- **V1.4 Cross-language Lab:** standardized C++/Python workload comparisons.
- **V1.5 Theme system:** complete tokenized dark mode.
- **V2:** separately designed AI explanation, complexity, diagnosis, and test-case
  features. These are intentionally absent from V1.
