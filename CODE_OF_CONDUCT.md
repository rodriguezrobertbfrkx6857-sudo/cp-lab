# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project. Be respectful, assume
good intent, critique ideas and code rather than people, and make room for learners.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not
accepted. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the
maintainers through the repository's security/contact channel.
